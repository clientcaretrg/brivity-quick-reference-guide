Cheatsheet: Lead Status

Leads vs. People, Tags, Intent, and Status — the core building blocks of a Brivity contact.

Source: Cheatsheet PDF

Leads vs. People

Leads

Contacts in active conversion.

THINK: Web Leads, Open Houses, Prospective Clients

People

Not actively buying or selling.

THINK: Sphere, Collaborators, Other Agents, Your Team

Tags

Tags are completely custom. Tag clients to help you easily find those contacts. Good example tags:

Sphere Investor Cash Buyer Closed 2018 Pie Giveaway ...or any short word / phrase

Intent

Brivity marks the Intent of leads as they come through from online sources. You can manually add or change intent.

Status

Set a status to identify when a contact wants to move.

NEWStatus unknown: a lead who has not yet been contacted.
UNQUALAttempted contact, no response.
HOTMoving in 1–3 months.
NURTUREMoving in 3–12 months.
WATCHMoving in 1 year or more OR sphere you're nurturing.
PENDINGClient currently under contract.
PAST CLIENTPrevious clients.
INACTIVENot responsive after a defined number of contact attempts (industry standard is 20 calls).
ARCHIVEStop all contact (DNC). Auto Plans, Listing Alerts, & Market Reports stopped.
TRASHBad/fake contact information. Auto Plans, Listing Alerts, & Market Reports stopped.
ACTIVE CLIENTAutomatically applied when a contact is linked to an Active or Pending transaction.
PROSPECTIVEAutomatically applied when a contact is linked to a Coming Soon or Pipeline transaction.
Many teams using the Lead Pond feature will have agents place 'Inactive' contacts into the pond as 'Unqualified'.
'Inactive', 'Archived', and 'Trash' statuses will hide people from list views unless you filter to find them.

Cheatsheet: Stages

Set a stage to track leads through the sales pipeline.

Source: Stages PDF

Lead Stages provide a structured way to categorize and track leads through the sales pipeline. By visually tracking where a lead stands, you can optimize your sales process, making adjustments where necessary.

The 13 stages, in order

  1. New
    The lead has just entered the system and awaits further action.
  2. Attempted Contact
    An initial outreach was made, but no connection was established yet.
  3. Spoke with Customer
    Successful communication with the lead.
  4. Appointment Set
    A scheduled meeting with the lead is on the horizon.
  5. Met with Customer
    The face-to-face or virtual meeting with the lead took place.
  6. Showing Homes
    Actively showcasing homes to potential buying leads.
  7. Listing Agreement
    The lead (typically a seller) has a formal listing arrangement.
  8. Active Listing
    The lead's property is now available in the market.
  9. Submitting Offers
    Buyer leads making property offers.
  10. Under Contract
    Property associated with the lead is currently in a contractual phase.
  11. Sale Closed
    A successful closure of a sales process.
  12. Nurture
    Leads that show potential for future engagements and need regular nurturing.
  13. Rejected
    Leads that are no longer a fit or have lost interest.

Brivity Database Standards

Best practices to keep the database worked efficiently and capitalize on every opportunity.

Source: Database Standards PDF
NEW

New Leads

New leads should be processed as soon as possible but definitely in the first 24 hours. Processing a new lead involves making the first phone call and then changing the status of the lead so it's no longer in a 'new' status.

TASKS

Reminders and Tasks

Priority 1 reminders — when a promise is made for a specific day to a contact in the database. "You're going to speak with your lender on Thursday? That's awesome! I'll call you next Friday."

Priority 2 reminders — when a promise is made for a timeframe to a contact in the database. "I'll follow up with you next month."

Priority 3 reminders — everything else where there is no promise to the contact.

Rule of thumb: every Hot, Watch, Nurture, and Past Client contact in the database should have a next due task established.
HOT

Hot Status Contacts (0–90 days out)

Contacted once per week at a minimum.

NURTURE

Nurture Status Contacts (3–12 months out)

Contacted once per month at a minimum.

WATCH

Watch Status Contacts (1 year or more out)

Contacted once per quarter at a minimum.

PAST CLIENT

Past Client Status Contacts

Should have a 'last interaction' logged within the last 30 days, or a future task/reminder of the next intentional interaction planned.

ALERTS

Listing Alerts

Every buyer prospect in the database should be set up to receive listing alerts. Every. Single. One. If there's an idea of a property the contact has looked at or inquired about, use that as the baseline. If unsure, use a generic listing alert for the market (often the largest city/cities in a price range around the average priced home). At least 50% of the database should have an active listing alert.

REPORTS

Market Reports

Every contact with an email address and a home address should be set up on a market report — including past clients, sphere of influence, and seller prospects. Every buyer lead should also have a market report showing the market for their area of interest, which will often mirror the listing alert being sent.

DAILY

Recent Website Visitors & Market Report Viewers

Should have a last interaction within the last 7 days or a next due task in the future. Unqualified recent website visitors and market report viewers should be called/texted daily.

DAILY

Dashboard & Message Center

The dashboard should be reviewed daily — home valuation requests, requested showings, and property questions from website visitors all surface here.

The message center should be reviewed daily too. Every text message from a prospect should get a reply, in the form of "statement, question."
